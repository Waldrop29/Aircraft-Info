  • Service Ceiling: 41,400 feet

  • Wingspan: 197 feet 10 inches (60.30 meters)

Cabin & Capacity

  • Passenger Capacity: 250 to 290 seats (typical 3-class) or up to 440 (max layout)
  • Cabin Width: Main: 17' 4" (5.28 m) - Twin aisle (Wide-body)

Fuel & Efficiency

  • Fuel Capacity: 39,000 US gallons (147,850 liters)
  • Efficiency Gain: Long-range capability with high parts commonality, dropping overall fleet operational costs relative to previous generation quad-jets
