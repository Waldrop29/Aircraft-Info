Airbus A380-800

Airbus Homepage A330 A350 A340

Engine & Propulsion

  • Engine Model: Rolls-Royce Trent 900 OR Engine Alliance GP7200
  • Thrust: 74,735 lbs of thrust per engine with the Engine Alliance GP7200 OR 75,152 lbs of thrust per engine with the Rolls-Royce Trent 900.
  • Bypass Ratio: 8.7:1 with the Rolls-Royce Trent 900 OR 8.8:1 with the Engine Alliance GP7200.

Performance

  • Maximum Cruise Speed: 515 knots (954 km/h / Mach 0.89)
  • Maximum Flap Operating Speed (Flaps Full): 182 KIAS
  • Maximum Range: 8,000 nautical miles (14,800 km)
  • Service Ceiling: 43,100 feet

Dimensions & Weight

  • Wingspan: 261 feet 8 inches (79.75 meters)
  • Height: 79' 0" (24.09 meters)
  • Length: 238' 7" (72.72 meters)
  • Maximum Takeoff Weight (MTOW): 1,267,658 lbs (575 tonnes)

Cabin & Capacity

  • Passenger Capacity: 525 to 545 seats (typical 4-class) or up to 853 (max layout)
  • Cabin Width: Main: 21' 4" (6.50 m) / Upper: 19' 0" (5.80 m) - Double deck (Wide-body)
  • LD3 Cargo Containers: 38 underfloor positions

Fuel & Efficiency

  • Fuel Capacity: 84,535 US gallons (320,000 liters)
  • Efficiency Gain: 15% to 20% lower operating costs per seat than the previous generation 747-400
