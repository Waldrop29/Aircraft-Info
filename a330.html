Engine & Propulsion

  • Engine Model: Rolls-Royce Trent 7000-72
  • Thrust: 72,000 lbs of thrust per engine

Performance

  • Maximum Cruise Speed: 496 knots (918 km/h / Mach 0.86)
  • Maximum Flap Operating Speed (Flaps Full): 180 KIAS
  • Maximum Range: 7,200 nautical miles (13,334 km)
  • Service Ceiling: 41,450 feet

Dimensions & Weight

  • Wingspan: 210 feet (64 meters)
  • Height: 55' 1" (16.79 meters)
  • Length: 208' 11" (63.69 meters)
  • Maximum Takeoff Weight (MTOW): 533,519 lbs (242 tonnes)

Cabin & Capacity

  • Passenger Capacity: 260 to 300 seats (typical 3-class) or up to 460 (max layout)
  • Cabin Width: 17 feet 3 inches (5.26 meters) - Twin aisle (Wide-body)
  • LD3 Cargo Containers: 33 underfloor positions

Fuel & Efficiency

  • Fuel Capacity: 36,743 US gallons (139,090 liters)
  • Efficiency Gain: 14% better fuel burn per seat than previous generation A330s
